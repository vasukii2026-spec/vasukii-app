## What it is

Vasukii is a self-custody crypto wallet and encrypted messaging app for Android. No accounts, no server holding your keys — everything sensitive is generated and encrypted on-device.

- **On-device wallet** — keys are generated and encrypted locally; Vasukii never sees them
- **Standard recovery phrase** — 12-word backup, compatible with MetaMask, Phantom, Trust Wallet, and other standard EVM wallets
- **Biometric unlock** — Face ID / fingerprint, backed by your device's secure hardware
- **Encrypted messaging** — direct messages and threads, end-to-end encrypted

## Install

This app isn't on the Play Store — it's distributed directly, free.

1. Open the [download page](https://vasukii2026-spec.github.io/vasukii-app/) on your Android phone
2. Tap **Download for Android**
3. Open the downloaded file and allow "install from unknown sources" when prompted
4. Open Vasukii — future updates arrive automatically inside the app, no need to redownload for most changes

## Non-custodial, by design

Your recovery phrase is shown to you once, during setup. Write it down somewhere safe — Vasukii has no way to recover it or your funds if it's lost.

**A note on staking and local law:** cryptocurrency and staking are restricted or banned in some countries. Vasukii doesn't check where you are or whether these features are legal for you — that's on you to confirm before using them.
