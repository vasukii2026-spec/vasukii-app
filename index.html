Installing it

Tap the download button above on your Android phone. Downloads straight to your device — no store, no account.

Open the downloaded file. Android will ask to allow installs from this source — that's expected for apps outside the Play Store.

Allow the install, then open Vasukii. Future updates arrive automatically inside the app — you won't need to redownload for most changes.

Non-custodial by design. Your recovery phrase is shown to you once, during setup — write it down. Vasukii has no way to recover it for you if it's lost.
